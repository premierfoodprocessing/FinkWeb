Fink: The Broken Links — Closed-Test Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 August 2026

This policy applies to the ad-free closed-test build of Fink: The Broken Links.

Data handled by the closed-test build

Fink stores player names, scores, Rescue Keys, Journey progress, saved games, audio preferences, and appearance preferences locally on the tester's device. This lets the game work offline. The closed-test build does not transmit this data to Fink or to third parties.

The closed-test build has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, account system, server, cloud save, purchases, or location collection. It does not sell or share personal data.

Data removal

Fink has no account and retains no server copy of game data. A tester can remove all locally stored Fink data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

Before public release

Before production, this policy must be published at a stable, publicly accessible HTTPS URL and updated with the developer's named privacy contact. The same URL must be linked in the app and the Google Play Console listing. Any future advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing, or cloud feature will require a policy and Google Play Data Safety review before it is enabled.
